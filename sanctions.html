Methodology

How the models work.

Every output on this page follows the same pipeline — aggregate, score, forecast, verify — so a number never appears without a source behind it.

01
Aggregate
25,000+ restricted-party entries are consolidated across OFAC SDN, EU, UK and other major lists, alongside a continuous timeline of enforcement developments — designations, seizures, penalties.
02
Score
Named circumvention corridors receive 0–100 intensity scores with Severe and High bands; shadow-fleet enforcement pressure is compressed into a composite index with vessel-exposure breakdowns by group and flag.
03
Forecast
The shadow-fleet index carries a 13-week forecast direction, and a secondary-sanctions risk read feeds a 7 / 30 / 90-day watchlist. Forecast Desk probabilities are calibrated model estimates.
04
Verify & publish
Weekly outputs cite URL-verified sources under a strict 7-day recency rule, forecasts resolve only via whitelisted authorities, and accuracy is tracked by Brier score.

New · Forecast Desk

Autonomous weekly sanctions forecasts

Each week the platform autonomously generates binary sanctions forecasts — will a designation land, will a package be adopted — and publishes them with calibrated probabilities.

  • Every question cites at least two verified sources.
  • Resolution happens only via whitelisted authorities — OFAC, EUR-Lex and peers.
  • Probabilities are calibrated model estimates, tracked by Brier score.
  • An anti-hallucination contract is enforced in code, not by convention.
