What is in every pack

The evidence file behind your decision

For sanctions and export-control lawyers, compliance teams and trade advisors. The legal assessment stays with you.

01
Entity resolution
The exact legal entity behind the name: LEI (GLEIF), registry number, country and status. Look-alike names and lapsed records are flagged, never guessed.
02
Ownership chain to 50%
Direct and ultimate parents from GLEIF, national registers such as Companies House, and filings. Every percentage is sourced or marked unknown.
03
List hits, by regime
The entity and every owner screened against the US Consolidated Screening List (Entity List, MEU, SDN and more), the EU, UK and UN lists, with match score and listing reference.
04
A lane per regime
Continue, verify, prepare licence or pause, separately for US-BIS, US-OFAC, EU, UK and UN, with the rule applied and the stake that triggered it.
05
Unknown-ownership flag
If a listed party sits in the chain and its stake cannot be established, the pack says so plainly and lists the documents that would settle the question.
06
Dated evidence log
Every lookup timestamped with its source URL, list versions with publisher date and hash, and what public data could not establish. PDF and CSV.
RegimeTest the pack applies
US BIS (EAR)50% or more in aggregate by Entity List, MEU or certain SDN parties, through intermediaries (BIS Affiliates Rule, scheduled to return on 10 November 2026 under the current Federal Register text).
US OFAC50% or more in aggregate by blocked persons (OFAC 50 Percent Rule).
EU50% or more ownership, or control, by listed persons; control indicators listed for you to assess.
UKMore than 50% of shares or votes, or control; stakes are not added up unless held jointly.
