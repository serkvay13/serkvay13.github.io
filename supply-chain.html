Methodology

How the models work.

Measure, explain, forecast, validate — the same pipeline behind every corridor number and every disruption probability on the platform.

01
Measure
Each of the eight chokepoint corridors gets separate container (short-cycle) and dry-bulk (long-cycle) cost-pressure readings, a transit-disruption %, and escalation, news, and sanctions intensity.
02
Explain
Causal network analysis maps how risk transmits across countries, while the NY Fed GSCPI — in sigma and percentile terms — anchors corridor readings in global supply-chain pressure.
03
Forecast
Calibrated disruption probabilities are issued at 2 / 4 / 8-week horizons per corridor; the Corridor Risk Explorer extends route-level outlooks to 30 / 90 / 180 days, with a scenario simulator for what-ifs.
04
Validate
Shock forecasts are backtested and Brier-scored against naive baselines, and every published claim passes event-level verification before any narrative is written.
