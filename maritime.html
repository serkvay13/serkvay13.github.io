Methodology

How the models work.

Watch, score, forecast, verify — from raw AIS movement to a probability you can hold the model to.

01
Watch
An AIS-based live operations watch tracks disruption % versus baseline at each chokepoint, while port incidents and navigational warnings are collected continuously.
02
Score
Shadow-fleet enforcement pressure is compressed into a composite index, with vessel-exposure analytics beneath it.
03
Forecast
Escalation probabilities are issued per corridor at 2 / 4 / 8-week horizons, the shadow-fleet index carries a 13-week direction, and the war-risk signal prices JWC listed-area changes over 6 weeks.
04
Verify & publish
The Maritime Weekly ships five URL-verified events with strategic analysis and a PDF edition; shadow-fleet, Hormuz, and three-corridors briefings are published on the Foresight Desk.
