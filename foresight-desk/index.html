THIS WEEK'S BRIEFING
Europe's Defence Architecture 2024–2026: Rearmament, Partnerships, and the Germany–Russia Confrontation
NERAI assesses the Germany–Russia confrontation as operating at the edge of Article 5 thresholds: a kinetic-contact incident carries 25–35% probability within 12 months and 40–50% within 36, against a 2–5% tail risk of direct war — while Europe's counterweight hardens: the €150bn SAFE instrument is disbursing, Germany's 2026 budget is €108bn (2.4% of GDP), and the Bundeswehr's first-ever military strategy sets a 460,000-force target with maximised readiness by 2029.

The architecture is real money now: the €150bn SAFE instrument (adopted 27 May 2025) is disbursing to 19 states with a 65% European-preference rule; Germany's 2026 budget is €108bn (2.4% of GDP) against €32bn in 2014, and the March 2025 debt-brake amendment makes the shift fiscally irreversible in the medium term.

Moscow is the escalator: Russia's deteriorating-relations signal toward Germany (18.7/100) runs at more than double Germany's toward Russia (8.4/100), and the Leipzig-Halle explosive drone of 4 August drew Berlin's sharpest response yet — formal attribution to Russian state entities and the Bonn consulate closure on 1 September.

The scenario grid: managed friction 50–60%, sharp kinetic incident with controlled de-escalation 25–35% (40–50% on 36 months), sustained campaign to Article 4/5 thresholds 10–15%, direct war 2–5% — midpoints sum to ~100, calibrated against 75 years of NATO–Russia base rates.

Türkiye's landing zone: a structured transactional partner through 2029 — NATO's second-largest army and Montreux gatekeeper, blocked from SAFE by Cyprus (October 2025) with Athens tying consent to the Aegean casus belli; a Greece–Türkiye maritime settlement (20–30% over three years) is the variable that most changes the map.

2029 is a deadline, not a forecast: the 'ready for war by 2029' framing is a readiness planning assumption — the Bundeswehr's first-ever military strategy (22 April 2026) sets a 460,000 combat-ready force target with maximised readiness by 2029; conflating the planning horizon with a prediction is this file's most common analytical error.
